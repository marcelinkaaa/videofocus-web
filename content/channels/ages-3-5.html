Preschoolers do best with channels that are slow enough to follow, teach something without trying too hard, and don't end every video by pushing the next one. These are channels we'd happily approve for a 3 to 5 year old.

How we picked: calm pacing, clear learning value, and a long track record. Channels change, so watch a couple of videos yourself before approving. In VideoFocus, approving a channel means its new uploads appear too.

  • Ms Rachel - Toddler Learning Videosages 1–4

    Speech and early-language focused songs and lessons, made by an early-childhood music teacher. Long videos, so set a daily limit.

  • Sesame Streetages 2–5

    Letters, numbers, feelings and kindness from the show that wrote the playbook. Also has calming and mindfulness clips.

  • Super Simple Songsages 2–5

    Gentle, uncluttered nursery songs and action songs. One of the least hectic music channels for little ones.

  • Numberblocksages 3–6

    Counting, adding and early math ideas, taught through characters made of blocks. Genuinely builds number sense.

  • Alphablocksages 3–6

    Phonics from the same team as Numberblocks. Letters team up to make words. Good alongside learning to read.

  • Bluey - Official Channelages 3–7

    Clips and minisodes of the show parents enjoy as much as kids. Imaginative play over flashy edits.

  • PBS KIDSages 2–6

    Clips from Daniel Tiger's Neighborhood, Wild Kratts, Alma's Way and more, from a public broadcaster.

  • Cosmic Kids Yogaages 3–8

    Story-led yoga adventures that get kids moving, plus short relaxation sessions for winding down.

  • Danny Go!ages 3–7

    High-energy movement and dance games. Best for rainy days when they need to burn energy, not right before bed.

  • Art for Kids Hubages 4–10

    A dad and his kids drawing step by step. Pause-and-draw along, so the screen leads to paper.

Questions parents ask

How much YouTube should a 3 year old watch?

The American Academy of Pediatrics suggests about one hour a day of high-quality programming for ages 2 to 5, ideally watched together. A daily limit that stops playback when time's up takes the negotiation out of it.

Are these channels on YouTube Kids too?

Most of them are. In VideoFocus you can approve them whether or not they're in the YouTube Kids catalog, which matters more as kids get older.
