By nine, most kids have outgrown YouTube Kids and want "real YouTube". The good news: some of the best channels on the platform are perfect for this age. These are ones we'd happily approve, so tweens get the good stuff without the recommendation feed.

How we picked: real learning value, creators with a long track record, and videos that make kids want to try something themselves. Most of these aren't made only for kids, so they may not be in the YouTube Kids catalog, but you can approve them in VideoFocus. Watch a couple of videos first.

  • SciShowages 10+

    Quick, well-researched science explainers from the team behind SciShow Kids. A natural next step.

Questions parents ask

Is YouTube Kids too young for a 10 year old?

For many kids, yes, and several of the best channels for this age aren't in its catalog. An allowlist app lets you approve "real YouTube" channels one at a time instead of opening the whole site.

What about gaming and Minecraft channels?

They vary a lot, even within one creator. Approve individual channels you've watched, and revisit the list every few months. In VideoFocus, removing a channel takes it out of the library immediately.
