The best LEGO, domino and maker channels for kids

Updated 23 November 2026 · by the parent who builds VideoFocus

Some kids don't want to watch stories, they want to watch things get built. These channels are for them: giant LEGO creations, domino runs that take weeks to set up, working machines and 3D-printed gadgets. The best part is what happens after: the bricks come out.

How we picked: builds kids can learn from and copy, calm and curious creators, and no shock-for-clicks. Watch a couple of videos before approving.

    LEGO builds and creative challenges, with techniques kids can copy at home.

  • Beyond the Brickages 7+@BeyondtheBrick

    Tours of huge fan-made LEGO creations and chats with the people who built them.

  • Hevesh5ages 7+@Hevesh5

    Giant domino setups and chain reactions, plus how she plans and builds them.

  • TheDominoKingages 7+@TheDominoKing

Questions parents ask

Is watching LEGO videos actually good for kids?

Building channels are some of the most "do something after" content on YouTube. Kids pick up techniques and ideas, then try them. Pairing a daily limit with a pile of bricks works well.

Are toy unboxing channels on this list?

No. We left out unboxing and haul channels on purpose: they're mostly advertising. Everything here is about building and how things work.
