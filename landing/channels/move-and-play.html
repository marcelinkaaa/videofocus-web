YouTube channels that get kids moving

Updated 25 November 2026 · by the parent who builds VideoFocus

Not all screen time has to be sitting still. These channels turn a video into a dance party, a yoga story or a game of freeze, so kids end up out of breath instead of glazed over.

How we picked: videos that get kids up and moving, simple enough to follow without a grown-up, and made by creators with a long track record. Some are high energy, so they're better for the afternoon than right before bed.

  • Cosmic Kids Yogaages 3–8@CosmicKidsYoga

    Story-led yoga adventures: kids become the characters and move through the story. Also has short relaxation sessions.

  • Danny Go!ages 3–7@DannyGo

    High-energy dance and movement games. Perfect for rainy days when they need to burn energy.

  • GoNoodleages 4–10@GoNoodle

    Dance breaks and movement songs made for classrooms, plus calming and breathing videos for afterwards.

  • The Laurie Berkner Bandages 2–6@TheLaurieBerknerBand

    Sing-and-move songs from a former preschool music teacher. Little ones jump, wiggle and freeze along.

  • Raffiages 1–6@Raffi

    Classic sing-along and action songs like "Shake My Sillies Out". Gentle, and fun to do together.

  • Koo Koo Kanga Rooages 4–8@koo__koo

    Silly dance-along songs from the duo behind many GoNoodle favourites. The channel now goes by "Koo Koo".

Questions parents ask

Does movement screen time count toward a daily limit?

That's up to you. Many families treat active videos more generously than sit-and-watch ones. In VideoFocus, the daily limit covers everything, so a simple approach is to set the limit with a dance break or two in mind.

Are these good before bed?

Most aren't: they're designed to raise energy. For the last stretch of the day, try our calm and bedtime picks instead. Cosmic Kids Yoga's relaxation videos are the exception.
