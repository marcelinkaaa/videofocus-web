The best science and engineering YouTube channels for teens

Updated 23 November 2026 · by the parent who builds VideoFocus

Teens can handle real depth, and YouTube has some of the best science and engineering teaching anywhere. These channels treat teenagers as smart people: big ideas, real builds, and the kind of videos that turn into a school project or a career interest.

How we picked: accurate, well-made and genuinely interesting, from creators with a long track record. These are made for a general audience, not kids, so the occasional video may cover mature topics. Watch a couple first.

  • Veritasiumages 13+

    Deep, beautifully made videos on physics, math and surprising science.

  • SmarterEveryDayages 12+

    An engineer explores how things work, often in slow motion.

  • Practical Engineeringages 12+

    How bridges, dams and city infrastructure work, with tabletop demos.

  • Stuff Made Hereages 13+

    Ambitious, funny engineering builds, from first idea to working machine.

  • Steve Mouldages 12+

    Clever experiments that explain everyday physics.

  • Technic Brick Powerages 10+

    LEGO Technic cars put through engineering challenges: ramps, gears and gearboxes.

  • Real Engineeringages 13+

    How planes, rockets and big machines are designed.

  • Everyday Astronautages 13+

    Rockets explained properly: how engines work, why launches go the way they do, with real launch coverage.

  • Tom Scottages 13+

    Short films about amazing places, engineering and the stories behind them. A huge back catalog to explore.

  • minutephysicsages 12+

    Physics ideas explained in quick hand-drawn animation.

  • CrashCourseages 12+

    Full courses in history, science and literature. Great for school projects.

Questions parents ask

Isn't an allowlist too strict for a teenager?

It depends on the teen. Many families use one for younger teens or for school devices, and widen it over time. Building the list together, and letting your teen suggest channels, makes it feel less like a lock and more like a library.

Can my teen still use YouTube for homework?

Yes: approve the channels their classes use, like CrashCourse. In VideoFocus you can also approve single videos a teacher sends without approving the whole channel.
