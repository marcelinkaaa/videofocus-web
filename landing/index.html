Why VideoFocus

How it works

Three steps to a focused library

Sign in with your email, set a passcode, add what you trust. Setup takes a couple of minutes.

01

You hold the passcode

Set a parent passcode and create a profile for each child. Kids can watch, but only you can add channels and videos, change limits, or open the Family dashboard.

02

Add the channels & videos you trust

Search YouTube from inside the app and approve whole channels or single videos for each child. Approve a channel once and its new uploads appear. Nothing else does.

03

Set screen time limits

Choose a daily limit for each day of the week and a bedtime. VideoFocus counts the minutes a video is actually playing and locks playback when time is up. Part of the Family Plan.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is VideoFocus the same as YouTube Kids?

No. VideoFocus is an independent app. YouTube Kids filters a kids catalog for you; VideoFocus starts empty and plays only the YouTube channels and videos you add, from all of YouTube. VideoFocus is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google. Full comparison.

Which YouTube channels can I add?

Any public YouTube channel or video. Search inside the app's parent area and approve whole channels or single videos for each child. A few videos are set by their creators to block playback outside YouTube; those won't play in any app.

Do new videos from an approved channel show up automatically?

Yes. Approving a channel unlocks its whole catalog, including new uploads, so you don't have to approve videos one at a time.

Does VideoFocus block YouTube Shorts?

There's no Shorts feed and no swiping in VideoFocus. If a channel you approve posts Shorts, those can appear among that channel's videos, like its other uploads.

Will my child see ads?

VideoFocus shows no ads of its own and has no trackers or third-party analytics. Videos play in YouTube's official player, which can show YouTube's own ads.

What ages is it for?

Toddlers to teens. Each child gets their own profile, library, limits and watch history, so a 4 year old and an 11 year old can each have the right channels.

How is it different from Screen Time or Family Link?

Screen Time can limit or block YouTube as a whole but can't choose channels. Supervised YouTube through Family Link can block channels but not allow only certain ones. VideoFocus is the allowlist; Screen Time keeps the YouTube app and browsers off the device. Screen Time · Family Link.

Does it work on Android or on the TV?

No. VideoFocus is for iPhone and iPad (iOS 17.5 or later), synced across your family's devices.

What does it cost?

Watching, searching and adding channels are free, always. The free version covers one child on one device. The Family Plan adds a profile for every child, every device, and screen time limits and bedtime for $3.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a two-week free trial.

Why does youtube.com need to be allowed on my child's device?

The official YouTube player loads from youtube.com. If you use Screen Time's website restrictions, add www.youtube.com. What that opens up, and how to keep it tight.
