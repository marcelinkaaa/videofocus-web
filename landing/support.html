Common questions

I forgot my parental passcode. On the passcode screen, tap Forgot PIN?. We'll email a one-time code to your account's email address; enter it and choose a new passcode. No need to reinstall, and nothing is lost.

A video won't play. VideoFocus plays videos in YouTube's own player, so the device must be allowed to reach youtube.com. If Screen Time or another filter blocks YouTube on your child's device, see why VideoFocus needs YouTube access for how to allow it. Some creators also turn off playback outside YouTube for individual videos. If a specific video keeps failing, email us the link.

How do I use VideoFocus on more than one device? Sign in with the same email on each device. Profiles, approved channels and videos, limits, and history sync between them. Using more than one device is part of the Family Plan.

What does the Family Plan include? More child profiles, use on more than one device, and daily screen time limits and bedtime. It's $3.99/month or $39.99/year in the US, with a two-week free trial for new subscribers. Watching, searching, and adding channels and videos are free on every plan. Compare plans.

How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose VideoFocus. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's Family dashboard. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

I already subscribed but the app shows the free plan. Open the Family dashboard and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you subscribed with. If that doesn't fix it, email us.

How do I delete my account? Tap your child's name at the top left, enter your passcode, scroll the Family dashboard to Account, and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all child profiles and data. Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that separately in your Apple ID settings.

Can I use VideoFocus on Android? Not yet. VideoFocus is currently iPhone and iPad only. If Android support is important to you, let us know — demand helps us prioritize.
