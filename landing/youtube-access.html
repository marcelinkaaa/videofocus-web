Allowing YouTube on your child’s device

Last updated September 2026

If VideoFocus shows “Allow YouTube on this iPad to play videos,” your device’s Screen Time settings are blocking youtube.com. Here is why VideoFocus needs it, precisely what allowing it opens up, and how to keep the rest of the device locked down.

Why VideoFocus needs youtube.com

VideoFocus plays every video through YouTube’s official embedded player. That is a deliberate choice, and it is the only one we are willing to ship.

YouTube’s API Terms of Service require that YouTube videos be played in their official player, and Apple’s App Store guidelines (5.2.3) prohibit apps from streaming other people’s video content without authorization. There are apps that extract raw video streams and play them in a custom player. That approach would sail straight past a content filter — and it breaks both sets of rules, puts creators’ view counts and revenue at risk, and can get an app pulled from the store overnight.

We took the compliant path instead. The cost of that choice is this one setup step: the official player loads from youtube.com, so the device has to be allowed to reach it.

How to allow it

  1. Open Settings › Screen Time.
  2. Tap Content & Privacy Restrictions › Content Restrictions › Web Content.
  3. Tap Add Website. If you use Limit Adult Websites, add it under the ALWAYS ALLOW heading. If you use Only Approved Websites, add it to the list directly.
  4. Enter www.youtube.com — that exact address is all VideoFocus needs.

Return to VideoFocus and play a video. If it still fails, force-quit the app and reopen it so the player starts a fresh connection.

What this actually opens up

We would rather tell you this plainly than have you discover it later. Allowing a website in Screen Time is not scoped to one app.

Worth knowing

  • It applies device-wide, not just to Safari. Every browser on iOS — Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Opera — runs on Apple’s WebKit engine, so they all follow the same allow list. So does any app that shows web pages inside itself.
  • It allows all of youtube.com, not only the videos you approved — including YouTube’s search, its recommendations feed, and Shorts. If your child has a browser, they can reach them.
  • It does not unblock the YouTube app. That stays controlled separately by Screen Time › Allowed Apps and by app age ratings.

How to keep the fence tight

The gap above closes almost entirely if the device has no browser on it. Most children’s iPads do not need one.

Recommended setup

  • Turn off Safari and any other browsers under Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions › Allowed Apps. VideoFocus keeps working without them.
  • Use Only Approved Websites rather than Limit Adult Websites. The device can then reach nothing but the handful of sites you list.
  • Prevent new app installs under Content & Privacy Restrictions › iTunes & App Store Purchases, so a browser cannot simply be re-downloaded.
  • Set a Screen Time passcode that is different from the device unlock code, and different from your VideoFocus parental passcode.
  • Keep your VideoFocus passcode set. It gates adding channels, changing time limits, and every other parent control in the app.

What VideoFocus never shows

Inside the app itself, the fence does not depend on Screen Time at all. VideoFocus has no YouTube search for your child, no recommendation feed, no autoplay into the next suggested video, and no comments. Children browse the channels you approved and the videos you added — nothing beyond that. They can search their own library to find something you already approved, and adding anything new is behind the parental passcode.

That is the whole point of the app: you choose, and your child explores freely inside what you chose.

Still not playing?

A handful of videos are set by their own creators to block playback outside YouTube. Those will not play in any app, including ours — you will see a message saying the owner turned off sharing.

If videos fail more broadly after following the steps above, email us at ventureinklabs@gmail.com with the device model and iOS version, and we will help.
